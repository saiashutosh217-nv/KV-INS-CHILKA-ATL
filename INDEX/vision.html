Our Atal Tinkering Lab

Our Vision

To foster curiosity, creativity, and imagination among young students and nurture them into innovative problem solvers of tomorrow.

Our Mission

To provide a hands-on, project-based learning environment where students can experiment, innovate, and develop solutions to real-world problems using STEM tools and technologies.

Facilities & Features Available

1. Electronic Components

Wide range of resistors, capacitors, sensors, motors, and breadboards for building electronic projects.

2. Computers & Laptops for Research

High-performance computers and laptops with internet access for coding, research, and simulation work.

3. Study with Projector

Smart classroom setup with projector and screen for interactive learning and presentations.

4. Detailed Class with ATL Instructor

Regular structured classes conducted by trained ATL instructors for concept clarity and project guidance.

5. Doubt Solving Time

Dedicated sessions for students to clear doubts, discuss ideas, and get personalized guidance from mentors.
