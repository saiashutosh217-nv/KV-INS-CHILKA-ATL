ATAL TINKERING LAB
1. Innovation Hub

The ATL Lab is a center for innovation and creativity. Students are encouraged to explore new ideas. They learn by designing and building projects. The lab promotes practical learning experiences.

2. Hands-on Learning

Students learn through experiments and activities. They work with real tools and equipment. Learning becomes more interactive and engaging. This helps in better understanding of concepts.

3. Electronic Components

The lab provides various electronic components. Students use sensors, motors, LEDs, and circuits. They create innovative electronic projects. This develops technical skills and creativity.

4. Computer & Laptop Facilities

Computers and laptops are available for research. Students learn coding and programming skills. They explore new technologies and software tools for innovation.

5. Project Based Learning

Students work on real-life problem solving projects. They design, prototype and test their ideas. This builds confidence and practical knowledge.

6. Future Ready Skills

The lab prepares students for careers in STEM. It develops critical thinking, teamwork and creativity needed for the future.
