Subash Chandra Jena

Vice Principal

Subash Chandra Jena is a distinguished educational leader who has dedicated his career to promoting excellence in education and student development. He believes that quality education is the foundation of a successful and progressive society. Through his visionary leadership, the school has continued to grow in academic performance, discipline, and overall student achievement. He strongly encourages students to develop critical thinking, creativity, and leadership qualities. His focus is not only on academic success but also on building strong moral values and responsible citizenship among learners. He actively promotes the integration of modern technology and innovative teaching methods in the classroom. Under his guidance, teachers are motivated to continuously improve their professional skills and adopt student-centered approaches to learning. He encourages participation in sports, cultural events, science exhibitions, and community service activities. His leadership style is based on collaboration, mutual respect, and continuous improvement. He works closely with parents, teachers, and students to ensure a supportive learning environment. His commitment to educational excellence has inspired countless students to achieve their goals. Through dedication, integrity, and vision, he continues to guide the institution towards greater success and recognition.
