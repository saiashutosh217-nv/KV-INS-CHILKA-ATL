Amit Tokas

Headmaster

Amit Tokas is a dedicated and passionate Headmaster who believes in providing students with a strong foundation for lifelong success. His commitment to discipline, academic achievement, and character development has made him a respected leader within the school community. He works tirelessly to ensure that students receive quality education in a safe and supportive environment. He encourages active participation in academics, sports, cultural programmes, and social activities. His leadership promotes confidence, responsibility, and teamwork among students. He collaborates closely with teachers and parents to monitor student progress and support continuous improvement. He believes that education should empower students to become responsible citizens and future leaders. Through his dedication and vision, he continues to contribute significantly to the growth and development of the institution.
